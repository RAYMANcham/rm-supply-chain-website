About

Interim leadership with a
consultant's clarity

I help organisations run a more reliable and margin-conscious supply chain — aligning planning, stakeholders and AI-enabled insights.

My focus: S&OP, demand planning, inventory optimisation, and automation that reduces manual workload while improving financial performance.

My services

How I work

Practical. Data-driven. People-focused.

I start with reality: constraints, trade-offs, and what teams can actually execute. Then I translate it into a clear cadence: roles, KPIs, decisions, and routines.

The goal is simple: fewer escalations, better forecast-to-supply alignment, and leadership visibility that drives action.

Diagnose

Quick scan of process, data, roles and decision bottlenecks.

Align

Stakeholders, KPIs and governance — so decisions stick.

Build

Templates, cadences and dashboards the team owns.

Transfer

Coaching + documentation so improvement remains after handover.
