What I Do & How I Work

Structured engagements for supply chain leaders who want clarity, not complexity.

01

Supply Chain Performance Scan

Diagnostic → Clarity → Roadmap

Before investing in tools, AI, or reorganization — understand where performance is actually leaking. The Supply Chain Performance Scan is a structured 2–4 week deep dive into your planning and decision architecture.

What I assess

  • Forecast accuracy, bias & volatility patterns
  • Service level vs inventory trade-offs
  • S&OP governance and decision flow
  • KPI ownership & accountability
  • Cross-functional friction points
  • AI & automation readiness

You receive

  • Board-ready executive summary
  • Planning maturity assessment
  • 90-day quick win plan
  • 12-month improvement roadmap
  • Prioritized AI use case shortlist

This is not theory. It is clarity you can act on.

02

Inventory & Margin Optimization

Working Capital → Service → Profitability

Inventory is not a buffer. It is a strategic decision. I help organizations reduce working capital while protecting — and often improving — service levels.

Focus areas

  • Safety stock recalibration
  • Slow mover & obsolescence reduction
  • Service vs inventory scenario modeling
  • Policy redesign (MOQ, batch sizes, buffers)
  • Volatility & lead-time impact analysis

Outcome

  • Reduced working capital exposure
  • Improved service stability
  • Clear trade-off visibility for leadership
  • Stronger financial-operational alignment

Optimization without damaging performance.

03

Executive Advisory & Planning Coaching

Clarity → Structure → Decision Discipline

Even experienced supply chain leaders need a second perspective. I act as an independent advisor and strategic sounding board for directors and planning leaders navigating complexity or transformation.

Typical topics

  • Is our S&OP truly decision-driven?
  • Is the team structure aligned with strategy?
  • Are we solving the right bottlenecks?
  • Should we invest in this planning tool or AI solution?
  • Why does execution feel misaligned despite strong KPIs?

Formats

  • Monthly advisory retainer
  • Quarterly strategic reviews
  • On-demand executive sparring

Independent thinking. Structured reasoning. Clear recommendations.
